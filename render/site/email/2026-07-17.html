Formula key
Net sales — Σ net_sales over REPORTED entities, converted once to the reporting currency at the seeded fixed rate. A door that has not posted is excluded, never zero-filled.
Comp % — (Σ comp-door TY ÷ Σ comp-door LY-aligned) − 1. LY-aligned = same fiscal week, same weekday; holiday codes override week alignment.
Plan attainment — Σ reported actual ÷ Σ plan at each brand's native plan grain. A week-planned brand reads WTD against the weekly plan; a brand with no plan reads 'no plan'.
AST / AUS / UPT — net ÷ transactions, net ÷ units, units ÷ transactions. AST is the field's name for AOV and AUS for AUR — one formula, two labels.
Conversion — transactions ÷ traffic, expressed as a percentage of visits and compared to LY in basis points. Stores only; a door with no traffic posted is UNAVAILABLE, never 0%.
Discounts / Returns — discounts ÷ gross and store-side returns ÷ gross. Net sales = gross − discounts − returns, asserted per row.
OMNI penetration — omni-attributed sales ÷ net sales, recognized bases only. These sales are already inside net sales; penetration is a share of the headline, not an addition to it.
Contribution to comp gap — (entity TY − entity LY-aligned) ÷ total comp LY-aligned. Named contributions plus 'all other' equal the headline gap exactly.
KPI tree — Sales = Traffic × Conversion × AST, AST = AUS × UPT. Driver contributions use sequential attribution in the fixed order traffic → conversion → AST, which telescopes to the gap exactly.
